Identity without phone or email

03 / 06

Local-first storage

Your chats are kept with you, not in a cloud. The server holds no archive of your conversations.

The other side of it: lose the device without a backup and the chats are gone too.

  • Chats and identityon this device
  • Backupa file under your password
  • Disappearing messagesremoved on a timer

04 / 06

Sealed delivery

The server works like a mailbox: it takes a sealed packet and holds it until your contact comes online.

A packet is kept for a day at most and deleted once delivered.

The server sees

  • Your IP address
  • When you send
  • The packet size

The server cannot see

  • Message text
  • Photos and voice
  • Your keys

05 / 06

Network: HTTPS and Tor

These are different things. HTTPS encrypts the connection but does not make you anonymous. Tor hides your address — at the cost of speed.

If hiding the fact and the place of your connection matters, HTTPS alone is not enough.

HTTPS Main pathEncrypted · not anonymous

YouServerContact

Fast. The contents are closed, but the server and your provider see your IP address and when you connect.

Tor Fallback pathHides your address

YouTorTorTorContact

Slower. The connection passes through several nodes, and your IP address is not visible to the other side. Where Tor is blocked, a WebTunnel bridge can help. In the beta this path is experimental.

Honestly

What CyberDog does not promise

  • Absolute anonymity: on the main path the server and your provider see your IP address.
  • Protection from your contact: they can photograph the screen or retell what they saw.
  • Protection of a compromised or unlocked device: whoever holds your phone reads your chats.
  • A finished product: this is a beta, and there has been no independent audit yet.
